Goal Planner: three design directions

Each direction shows the same day: Home, a goal page, and Focus mode. They all start from your own ideas (glowing link view, tree and climb, the liquid timer). Pick what you like from each one. Mixing them is fine.

A. Constellation

Every goal is a star. Each finished task lights a small star, and the lines show what feeds what. Your year goal becomes a picture you fill in over time. Built from your "Link view (futuristic)" idea and the Black hole theme.

Check these apps first

  • Sky Guide: star maps, tap a star to see its lines
  • Forest: progress you can see grow
  • Structured: a clean day timeline

Colors

Night Starlight Violet Done glow Lines

Fonts

Syne for titles (wide and unusual), Manrope for everything else.

The one bold thing

The sky at the top of Home. Everything else stays quiet.

B. Trail

Goals as a climb. The year goal is the summit, and each day is a waypoint on the way up. Progress is height gained, so a good week looks like a steep climb. Built from your tree idea (trunk, branches, leaves), turned into a route.

Check these apps first

  • Strava: elevation profile, weekly totals
  • AllTrails: map with route and waypoints
  • Things 3: calm, clear task rows

Colors

Sage Forest ink Deep pine Trail marker Contour

Fonts

Bricolage Grotesque for titles (chunky, outdoorsy), Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading (made for easy reading).

The one bold thing

The orange trail marker: it's always on the next thing to do.

C. Vessel

Every goal is a glass. Finishing a task pours into the goal it feeds, and that glass pours into the next one up. It's your liquid tube timer idea, used for the whole app: your day is a glass you fill.

Check these apps first

  • Waterllama: filling a vessel, playful but calm
  • Apple Fitness rings: daily progress at a glance
  • Tiimo: a soft, visual day plan

Colors

Mist Deep water Teal Water Why (coral)

Fonts

Instrument Serif for big numbers and your "why" (soft and personal), Inter Tight for everything else.

The one bold thing

The big glass on Home, gently moving.
